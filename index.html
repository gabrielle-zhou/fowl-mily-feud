How to play

1

Face-off

One player from each team steps up, hands flat on the table. The host reads the question out loud. First to grab the spoon answers. If it isn't the #1 answer, the other player gets a shot. The higher answer on the board wins. If neither is on the board, the next pair steps up.

2

Play or pass

The face-off winner's team chooses: play the board themselves, or pass it to the other team.

3

Guess the board

The team in control answers one player at a time, down the line. No conferring. Every answer that's not on the board is a strike.

4

Three strikes

Three strikes and the other team gets one chance to steal. They huddle and give a single answer.

5

The steal

If the steal answer is on the board, the stealing team takes every point in the bank. If not, the team in control keeps them.

6

Winning

Clear the board and your team banks the points. Highest total after the last round wins the feud.
